How it works

Five steps, no data connection required.

1

Open the Powu app

Launch Powu on any Android phone — no setup beyond installing it.

2

Enter payment details

Choose UPI ID, phone number, QR code, or account transfer.

3

Powu dials *99#

The app initiates the USSD service in the background on your behalf.

4

Follow your bank's prompts

Respond to the instructions sent by your bank or UPI service.

5

Authorize with your UPI PIN

Confirm the transaction — your bank completes it, Powu never touches the funds.

⚠️ Availability depends on your bank, mobile network, and the services supported through *99#.
